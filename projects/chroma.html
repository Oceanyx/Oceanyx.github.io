Why I built it

What something felt like and what actually happened often don't match, and the gap is hard to see when your reflections are scattered across a notes app and your memory. Chroma started as a way to make that gap visible.

I'm less interested in journaling for its own sake than in the mechanism behind a conclusion: how you got from something that happened to what you decided it meant. If you record enough small reflections, patterns start to show up across them that you wouldn't spot in any single entry.

Philosophy

A few ideas shaped what Chroma does and what I left out.

  1. Keep feeling and fact apart

    Every observation gets a felt reflection and a verifiable one. The point isn't to decide the feeling is wrong. It's to see where the two diverge.

  2. Describe before you interpret

    Behavior and framing unlock only after you've logged 5 and 15 reflections. You write down what happened and what you did before you're offered a frame for what it means.

  3. Patterns, not verdicts

    Chroma doesn't tell you what your entries mean. It gives you a canvas where links, tension and support between them become visible, and you draw the conclusions.

  4. Your data stays with you

    A tool for examining your own thinking shouldn't send it anywhere. There are no accounts and no server.

  5. Small on purpose

    I left out search, sync and undo until I know whether the core idea holds up. More features wouldn't help answer that.

How it works

Each observation, action or intention becomes a node, called a planet, on an infinite canvas you can pan and zoom. You attach reflections to a planet along up to four dimensions, which appear as moons orbiting it.

Felt

What it felt like in the moment. Available from the start.

Verifiable

What was externally true. Available from the start.

Behavioral

What you did. Unlocks after 5 reflections in total.

Framing

The pattern or frame it seems to fit. Unlocks after 15.

Planets can be grouped into constellations and linked with typed relationships: followed, caused, triggered, enabled, contradicts or resolved. You can also mark two planets as in tension or in support of each other, and mark individual reflections as supporting or contradicting one another.

Where it stands

The core loop works: create an observation, reflect on it across the dimensions, and connect it to others. It's early, but you can use it today.

Working now

  • Observation, action and intention nodes on an infinite canvas
  • Reflections across all four dimensions
  • Tension and support marking
  • Constellations and typed relationships
  • JSON export and import

Left out for now

  • Accounts, sign-in and cross-device sync
  • Voice capture
  • Search and filtering
  • A visible settings panel
  • Undo/redo and more keyboard shortcuts

I left these out on purpose. I want to find out whether the core idea holds up before adding more.

Privacy

Chroma makes no network requests. Everything you create is stored in your browser's IndexedDB and stays on your device. There are no accounts and no server. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it all, so use Export if you want a backup.
