Chroma

Live

A local-first tool for mapping how you make sense of your own experiences — treating each thing you notice as a small planet, and your reflections on it as moons in orbit.

Try Chroma View source on GitHub

Why I built it

Perception is noticeably fickle — the way something felt in the moment and what was actually true often don't match, and that gap is easy to miss when reflections stay scattered across a notes app or your own memory. Chroma started as an attempt to make that gap visible.

The goal isn't just journaling for its own sake. It's giving people a way to notice not only what they conclude about their own thoughts and behavior, but the mechanism behind how they got there — and to let real patterns surface across many small reflections instead of staying invisible.

How it works

Every observation, action, or intention becomes a node — a "planet" — on an infinite pan-and-zoom canvas. You attach reflections to it along up to four dimensions, shown as moons orbiting the planet:

Felt

What it felt like in the moment — open from the start.

Verifiable

What was actually, externally true — also open from the start.

Behavioral

What you did. Unlocks after 5 total reflections — intentional pacing, not a bug.

Framing

What pattern or frame it fits. Unlocks after 15 reflections.

Planets can be grouped into constellations, and linked with typed relationships — followed, caused, triggered, enabled, contradicts, resolved — or simply marked as being in tension or in support of one another. Individual reflections can also be marked as supporting or contradicting each other directly.

Where it stands

The core loop — create an observation, reflect on it across dimensions, connect it to others — works today. It's an early but genuinely usable tool, not a mockup.

Working now

  • Observation / Action / Intention nodes on an infinite canvas
  • Reflections across all four dimensions
  • Tension & support marking
  • Constellations and typed relationships
  • JSON export / import

Not built yet — on purpose

  • Accounts, sign-in, or cross-device sync
  • Voice capture / dictation
  • Search and filtering
  • A visible settings panel
  • Undo/redo, fuller keyboard shortcuts

These are deliberately out of scope for now — the goal is to test whether the core idea holds up before the surface area grows.

Privacy

Chroma makes no network requests of any kind. Everything you create lives in your browser's IndexedDB and never leaves your device — there's no account system and no server, because there's nowhere for the data to go. Clearing your browser's site data will delete it, so use Export if you want a backup.
