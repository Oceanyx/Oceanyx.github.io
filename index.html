What I'm building

Chroma

Live

A local-first tool for mapping how you make sense of your own experiences.

It's live and usable today, with more features on the way. Chroma treats each thing you notice or do as a small planet. You attach reflections to it — what you felt, what you did, what's actually verifiable, and what pattern it fits — as moons orbiting that planet. Planets link into constellations, and can be marked as supporting or in tension with each other. The idea is to make the gap between how something felt and what actually happened a little easier to see, and to let patterns surface across a lot of small reflections over time.

It runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no server — nothing ever leaves your device.

Local-first IndexedDB Pan & zoom canvas No tracking
