What I'm making

Chroma

Live

A local-first tool for mapping how you make sense of your own experiences.

Chroma turns the things you notice into a small universe. Every observation becomes a planet, and your reflections on it — what you felt, what actually happened, what pattern it fits — become moons in orbit around it. Planets link into constellations, and can be marked as supporting or contradicting each other.

It's live and worth trying today, not just a proof of concept. And it never talks to a server — everything stays in your browser, so nothing you write here ever becomes someone else's data.

Rift

A real-time messaging platform built around low-latency delivery and proper session auth.

Rift is a Socket.io-backed chat platform with channel organization and secure, session-based authentication. The more interesting part was underneath: tuning the PostgreSQL queries so message history stays fast to pull even as conversations grow, instead of just throwing more indexes at it later.

About

I'm drawn to problems that don't already have an obvious shape — the kind where you have to figure out what you're even building before you can build it. Right now a lot of that comes out as code, but this site isn't just a home for software. It's wherever the current thing happens to be.

I came to it sideways — an English degree, a stint coaching competitive chess, and then a software engineering program at App Academy that turned "figure out the shape of the problem first" into an actual job description. The chess habit stuck: I still think in terms of position and pattern before I think in terms of syntax.

That's not just a nice metaphor, either — I'm a USCF-rated player (peak 1827 standard, 1907 blitz) and spent a stretch ranked top 1% globally in League of Legends and TFT, captaining a five-person squad through regional tournaments. Different games, same discipline: read the position fully before you commit to a line.
