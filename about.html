Who I am

I'm Brian. I've taught chess and tutored math to kids for over four years. I also have an English degree, some mechanical engineering coursework, and I finished the software engineering program at App Academy.

The common thread for me is explaining things step by step. Whether it's a chess endgame or a bug in a database query, I like working out what the actual problem is and then breaking it into pieces small enough to follow.

Teaching

I've run weekly chess classes of 15 to 20+ students. Each lesson is built around one skill, followed by a practice puzzle and supervised play, and I check each student's understanding individually before moving on. The material ranges from basic tactics and endgames to opening principles and pawn structure.

I also tutored a small group of 4 or 5 elementary school students in math, working through arithmetic word problems and giving individual help whenever someone got stuck.

Some of my chess students went on to enter Bay Area tournaments, many for the first time, and I went along to watch them play.

Software

I finished the software engineering program at App Academy in March 2025. My projects are on the home page. Chroma is the one I'm proudest of: I came up with the design myself and built it with the help of AI coding tools.

Other work

I've also worked as a barista and cashier. That meant handling cash and payments, tracking inventory, training new staff, and keeping up through shifts of 100+ orders.

Outside of work

I play tournament chess. My US Chess rating peaked at 1827 in standard and 1907 in blitz. I also captained a five-person League of Legends team in tournaments, which meant coordinating schedules and strategy, and I've ranked in the top 1% globally in League of Legends and TFT.

I play a lot of different games on Steam and watch a lot of anime. I'm also interested in psychology and philosophy, and I'd like to write about them here at some point.
